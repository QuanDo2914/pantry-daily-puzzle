# Quality V3 Production-readiness Recommendation

## Recommendation

**Stop at the approval gate. Do not integrate Quality V3 into production yet.**

The batch is mathematically sound and daily rotation is dramatically better than V2, but Hard misses the catalog-coverage and frequency targets. The proposed Hard scoring catalog also changes 149 definitions by retaining four primary ingredients and moving later garnishes/seasonings out of scored inventory. Those changes are explicit and isolated, but they require culinary review and approval.

## Targets achieved

- 200/200 puzzles independently pass exhaustive validation.
- Exactly 100 unique solution groups per mode.
- Zero duplicate mathematical structures.
- Medium recipe coverage: **120/150** target achieved.
- Medium maximum frequency: **23**, below the 25-review threshold.
- All 15 families appear in both modes.
- Median adjacent overlap: **0** in both modes.
- Hard average adjacent overlap: **0.30**, below the target of 3.
- Hard longest streak: **4**, close to the preferred 3 and far below V2's 37.
- No Hard transition reuses at least half of the prior day's cards.
- Hard difficulty improved: 7.48 average strict near misses and 5.32 average deduction depth.
- Hard uses a 12–16 pantry ceiling rather than 18 in this proposal.

## Targets not achieved

- Hard recipe coverage: **116/150**, below 120.
- Hard maximum frequency: **41**, above the ideal 20 and review threshold 25.
- Hard rolling repetition peaks at 6/7, 11/14, and 17/30 appearances for one recipe.
- Hard uses a seven-family floor, not the originally requested eight.
- Pantry ranges and the proposed scoring catalog remain unapproved.
- No real-player timing or difficulty study has been performed.

## Smallest reasonable next catalog revision

Do not run another broad optimizer first. Curate approximately 20–30 recipe definitions in three or four overlap clusters, then measure once:

1. Tomato/pasta/pizza: connect pasta, soup, bake, flatbread, and comfort-food families while keeping dough or pasta identity ingredients.
2. Seafood/rice/vegetables: connect salmon, shrimp, rice dishes, stir-fries, soups, and bowls through garlic, lemon, broccoli, rice, and spinach.
3. Breakfast/bread/potato: connect egg dishes, sandwiches, casseroles, and vegetable skillets.
4. Noodles/stir-fries/soups: connect noodles across multiple families with cabbage, carrot, mushroom, soy sauce, and protein.

Each proposed adjustment should retain four recognizable primary ingredients, document any removed scored garnish, and be accepted only if it increases valid candidate boards for an underused recipe without creating a universal decoy. This targeted review is preferable to approving the current 149-definition simplification wholesale.
